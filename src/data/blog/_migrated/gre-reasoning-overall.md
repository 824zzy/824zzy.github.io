### First Passage Experience

- plant **communities**: community

- more **susceptible** than: susceptible to influence

- **inconsistent** theory: inconsistent

- resource-release **mechanisms**: methods

- **ecological** correlates: ecological

- no **necessary** relationship: necessary

- this may **arise from** sth: due to
